Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Talby ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we protect it. By using Talby, you agree to the practices described here.

Talby is currently in final development and has not yet launched on the App Store. This policy describes how the app will handle data once live, and will be reviewed before public launch.

1. Information we collect

Account information: email address, and any name you provide, when you sign up or join the waitlist.

Financial data: if you connect a bank account, Talby receives read-only transaction data through our banking partners, Plaid (US and Canada) and TrueLayer (UK and EU). We never receive or store your online banking login credentials; those are handled entirely by Plaid or TrueLayer under their own security standards.

Statements you upload: if you paste text or upload a photo of a statement instead of connecting your bank, that content is processed to detect billing issues and is not retained longer than needed to deliver that result.

Usage data: basic app analytics such as feature usage and crash reports, used only to improve the product.

2. How we use your information

  • To detect duplicate charges, price increases, and unauthorized subscriptions
  • To draft and send recovery emails on your behalf, only when you choose to send one
  • To calculate your budgeting and financial health insights within the app
  • To connect you with our CPA partner, only if you choose to book a tax service
  • To communicate with you about your account, the waitlist, or app updates

3. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal or financial data to third parties
  • We never initiate a payment, transfer, or withdrawal from your account; access is read-only
  • We never store your bank login credentials

4. Data security

Financial data and session tokens are encrypted using the iOS Keychain (AES-256). Bank connections are handled through Plaid and TrueLayer, both of which are independently audited and widely used by major financial apps.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app, or by emailing support@gettalbyapp.store. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request, except where we're required to retain records by law.

6. International users

Talby is designed to comply with GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), PIPEDA (Canada), LGPD (Brazil), and APPI (Japan) for users in those regions. If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to certain processing, by contacting us at the email above.

7. Third-party services

Talby relies on a small number of trusted service providers to operate: Plaid and TrueLayer for bank connections, Apple for subscription billing, and a licensed CPA partner for optional tax services. Each handles data under its own privacy policy in addition to this one.

8. Children's privacy

Talby is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Talby develops. Material changes will be communicated by email or an in-app notice before they take effect.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@gettalbyapp.store
